Every section was refitted along the sweep of \cref{sec:sweep}; \cref{tab:kappa-star} gives each finding of \crefrange{sec:intrinsic-response}{sec:relocation} its breakdown point, in two groups. The \emph{allocation checks}, that $\vz$ keeps the cycle and $\vw$ the niche, are favoured by the model's design, so their breakdown points say less than those of the \emph{biological claims}.

Six cells of the table break within the grid, in three contrasts: the tumour axis (the response's ordering by distance to the tumour) on the lung FFPE and FF sections, its lean toward ligand--receptor genes on the FF section, the earliest break ($\kappa^\star = 0.2$), and the relocation's margin over its spill-over part on both FFPE sections and the serial section. Every other finding holds to $\kappa = 0.4$ wherever it is read. On the serial section every contrast replicates the core's except the relocation's margin over its spill-over part, which replicates only up to $\kappa = 0.3$. Two contrasts first in the graded set were moved to trajectories after reading, by the rule of \cref{sec:sweep} (\suppref{app:sweep}).

A planted control checks the ordering where the answer is known (\suppref{app:S-planted-spillover}): in simulation, a contrast made only by spill-over is no finding when none is planted and breaks at $\kappa^\star = 0.05$ and $0.1$ when $0.1$ and $0.2$ are planted, one grid step early (conservative), while a genuine response contrast in the same worlds holds through $\kappa = 0.2$.

Of the spill-over forms the sweep leaves open, several were refitted on the primary section, three seeds each (\suppref{app:sensitivity}): per-cell and per-gene spill-over rates move no read by more than $1.3$ seed standard deviations, but a fixed false-positive floor lowers the relocation read from $0.65$ to $0.50$.
