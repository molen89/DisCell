\subsection{Lineage Labels}
\label{app:lineage}

The fitted labels are lineage-level on every section (\cref{sec:setup}): states (proliferating, activated, hypoxic) and locations (tumour- or stroma-associated) are merged into their lineage; lineages and developmentally distinct sub-lineages are kept. Mappings were fixed before any model was refitted. On the ovarian FFPE section the eighteen curated classes become twelve (\cref{tab:lineage-ovarian}). The class named as a SOX2-OT$^+$ tumour subtype is mostly not tumour (a median of $27$ transcripts against $333$ for tumour cells, three quarters with no SOX2-OT count, a pooled profile mostly of macrophage expression) and is folded into Unassigned. The class named as malignant cells lining a cyst expresses mesothelial markers (CALB2, PRG4, BNC1, PDPN, UPK3B) and lacks the tumour markers (EPCAM, PAX8, ESR1), so it becomes its own lineage. The tumour- and stroma-associated fibroblasts are merged, since the cancer-associated fibroblast programme that separates them is a state; the two endothelial classes are merged too, although what separates them (a tip-cell state against a weakly venous identity) is less clear. On the lung TMA the thirty-five curated classes become thirty: the state classes are reassigned cell by cell to the sub-lineage their markers indicate (right for about three cells in four), and the myofibroblasts are split about evenly between alveolar and adventitial fibroblasts. On the lung FFPE and fresh-frozen sections each unsupervised cluster is assigned the lineage its markers indicate (on the TMA's vocabulary for lung); immune clusters next to tumour that carry some tumour signal keep their immune lineage, since that signal is the spill-over the model absorbs. The cluster-to-lineage mappings accompany the code.

\subsection{Baselines}
\label{app:baselines}

\Cref{tab:baselines} gives the configuration of each comparison method. Every method runs at its published defaults, on our pruned Delaunay graph where it accepts one (resolVI and MintFlow build their own), and reads the lineage labels if it reads a label (SIMVI does not). Every comparison is on whole sections; a method that cannot be fitted to a whole section is reported as such, with the measured reason, never run on a window. Cellina removes a spatial-domain label adversarially; our sections carry none, so its main row runs without the adversary. It also runs with the adversary given our niche label (clusters of neighbour composition, $K = 10$, fitted on training cells), which matches what our probe grades and is its best case on the probe, not its published setting; on its own neighbour graph, on the TMA core and its serial section; and, for \cref{tab:cellina-cf}, refitted on the training tiles and decoded at its posterior mean. \Cref{tab:probe,tab:battery} give every method's values. MintFlow's reconstruction is not reported: an export error in our pipeline passed MintFlow's split of each cell's observed counts, which sums back to those counts, in place of a decode, so the stored read scored each cell against its own counts. MintFlow is being refitted with a corrected export, and all its reads will come from the new fits \pending{MintFlow refits with the corrected export}.

\paragraph{Reconstruction on one basis.} The methods differ in likelihood: DISCELL's is a multinomial given the cell's total, the comparison methods use their own count families, and SIMVI has no count decoder. The battery therefore scores every method the same way: its decoded expected counts are renormalised per cell to a probability vector $\vp_i$, and the read is the held-out per-count log-likelihood $\ell_i^{-1}\sum_g x_{ig}\log p_{ig}$, averaged over cells. A method's dispersion or zero-inflation parameters do not enter, so the read compares decoded mean profiles, not the methods' own likelihoods.
% source: discell/experiments/baseline_battery.py reconstruction() (decoded rate renormalised per cell) and discell/model/metrics.py held_out_reconstruction (mean over cells of sum_g x log p / total); identical function for DISCELL's rows.

\paragraph{Cellina variants.} Given our niche label as its domain, Cellina's composition residual does not consistently fall: the nonlinear residual falls on three sections of five and rises on lung and the TMA core, by at most $2.0$ points either way; the linear one rises on four of five; NMI with type and cycle $R^2$ fall on every section. On its own neighbour graph Cellina keeps less cycle state than DISCELL (cycle $R^2$ $0.39$ on the TMA core and $0.31$ on its serial section, against DISCELL's $0.48$--$0.51$ and $0.49$--$0.50$) at a nonlinear composition residual similar to DISCELL's on the core ($4.2\%$ against $3.5$--$4.8\%$) and higher on the serial section ($8.3\%$ against $4.0$--$4.8\%$); against Cellina on our graph, its residual is slightly higher and its cycle $R^2$ higher (\cref{tab:probe,tab:battery}).

The image model KRONOS and its successor KRONOS2 \citep{kronos2025} are released under CC BY-NC-ND~4.0, for non-commercial academic research and after registration; we use them as distributed by their authors, for that purpose, and redistribute neither. % Licences verified 2026-10-01 (package metadata): scvi-tools BSD-3, MintFlow BSD-3, Cellina BSD-3; simvi 0.1.2 declares none.

No new data are released.

\subsection{What the Model Needs}
\label{app:inputs}

Every fit in this paper is to a section profiled with Xenium Prime 5K, from whose output the model uses five inputs: the counts per cell; one position per cell (the centroid of the segmented cell), from which the graph, the kernel, the tiles and the image fields are built (\cref{app:graph}); the pixel size; a type label per cell, from a curated annotation or from clusters mapped to lineages (\cref{app:lineage}); and the four-channel morphology image. Segmented polygons are not required; they served only to choose the radius of the disc that removes the cell from its image field, which a new tissue or platform would need to recheck (\cref{app:image}), and for the graph and mask comparisons. Transcript coordinates, nucleus boundaries and the post-run H\&E image are not used; cell-cycle genes enter a diagnostic only (\cref{sec:selection}). Implicitly, counts must be assigned to segmented cells at single-cell resolution, since the spill-over term models transcripts misassigned between neighbouring cells; one fit is to one contiguous section; and the multinomial given each cell's total suits the hundreds to about a thousand counts per cell of in situ data (\cref{sec:generative}). No other assay has been tested.
