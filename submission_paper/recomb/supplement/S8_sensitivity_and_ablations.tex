\subsection{The Spill-over Sweep in Full}
\label{app:sweep}

\Cref{tab:kappa-sweep} gives the diagnostics of every fit of the sweep, drawn in \cref{fig:kappa-sweep}, and \cref{tab:breakdown-traj} the readouts that are reported as trajectories across the grid rather than with a breakdown point: magnitudes, positive under any fit, descriptions of what the invariance leaves, the marker-pair contrast, which measures the decode rather than the spill-over correction (\cref{app:response}), and the relocation prediction's margin over its programme part, which is zero at $\kappa = 0$ by construction, since no spill-over is modelled there. The breakdown points of the claimed contrasts, drawn in \cref{fig:breakdown-data}, are listed in \cref{tab:breakdown}. All hold across the grid except the signalling-gene share on the fresh-frozen section, which breaks down at $\kappa = 0.2$, and the relocation prediction's margin over its spill-over part, which breaks down at $\kappa = 0.4$ on the primary section and $0.3$ on the lung section. Its margin over its programme part is positive in every seed on every section from $\kappa = 0.05$ to $0.2$, by $0.02$ to $0.11$ in $R^2$ (mean over seeds); it stays positive up to $\kappa = 0.4$ on the lung and fresh-frozen sections and turns negative on the primary section from $\kappa = 0.3$ and, in the mean, on the TMA core at $0.4$ (\cref{tab:breakdown-traj,sec:results-sweep}). As $\kappa$ grows, more of the resemblance between neighbours is assigned to the mixture, and Moran's I of the intrinsic state falls on every section (from $0.118$ to $0.072$ on the primary section, $0.064$ to $0.038$ on the lung section, $0.360$ to $0.302$ on the fresh-frozen section and $0.076$ to $0.038$ on the TMA core), while that of the response changes by at most $0.05$. The within-type share of the response's variance, the effective number of programmes and the overlap of the programme spaces with the reference fit change little along the grid, the overlap staying at $0.76$ to $0.92$ on average at every grid value, comparable to the overlap between seeds at the operating point, whose lowest member is $0.72$ to $0.84$, with a mild decline at $\kappa = 0.4$; the relocation read's fraction of the ceiling is lower at $\kappa = 0$ than at the operating point on every section, peaks at $\kappa = 0.05$ to $0.2$, and falls beyond, most on the primary section, where it reaches $0.35$ at $\kappa = 0.4$.

\paragraph{The claimed contrasts and the rules.} The breakdown point is computed for the contrasts the results claim: the asymmetry of cell-cycle information between $\vz$ and $\vw$; the response's information about the niche above its floor; the two per-cell relocation reads, Read A and the twin margin (\cref{app:readouts}), against their references; the relocation prediction against its spill-over part; the signalling-gene share of the response; and, on the primary section, the alignment of the response with a known tumour axis. That is six contrasts per section and seven on the primary section; on the held-out serial section only the cycle asymmetry is read (\cref{tab:breakdown}). \pending{breakdown gaps queue: the families become seven contrasts on each whole-slide section (the tumour axis added on lung and FF), six on the TMA core and six on the serial section, read through the core's fits; update this paragraph and the serial-section rule below} Rankings are read through pairwise differences, each with its own breakdown point. No readout was designated primary in advance: the final sweeps were preceded by development sweeps, so any such set would have been chosen with knowledge of earlier results. The interval of a contrast is for its mean over seeds, from a spatial block bootstrap over tiles within each seed's fit, two-sided and Bonferroni-adjusted over the claimed contrasts of the section. Findings are section-level. Where two serial sections of the same tissue are available, a tissue-specific finding is claimed only if it holds on both; only the TMA core has one, and among the claimed contrasts only the cycle asymmetry is read on it, so the rule applies to that contrast alone. A property of the method, such as whether a readout moves with $\kappa$ at all, is required on every section. Some readouts move with $\kappa$ by construction: as $\kappa$ grows, more of the resemblance between neighbours is assigned to the mixture and the within-type spread of the response shrinks, so a decline along the grid is mechanical, whereas a change in the direction of a programme or in which latent a target aligns with is not. Gene programmes are identified only up to rotation and per-column constants (\cref{app:symmetries}), so raw loadings and probe directions are never compared across runs or seeds. And the interval at a grid point is a sampling statement that narrows with more cells, whereas the trajectory across the grid is not a confidence interval and does not narrow, because the confound it expresses is not a sampling problem.

\subsection{Sensitivity to the Assumptions}
\label{app:sensitivity}

\Cref{tab:sensitivity} refits the final configuration with one setting changed at a time, on the primary section and, for two of the families, on the TMA core and the fresh-frozen section, and gives each arm's move in units of the final configuration's standard deviation over its three seeds; \cref{fig:sensitivity} draws the moves. The arm without the intrinsic path, fitted on all four sections, is read against the final fits' range over seeds instead and is not in the table. An arm has two or three seeds, and the final configuration's spread over three seeds is itself a rough estimate, so moves of one or two such units are within what a change of seed can produce. Nothing is adopted from these arms.

\paragraph{The form of the spill-over.} In the three arms the spill-over rate is set per cell, in proportion to the ratio of its neighbours' depth, or density, to its own, capped, or per gene, in proportion to the gene's share of transcripts detected outside nuclei, each at the same mean rate as the section-wide $\kappa$. No read moves by more than $1.3$ standard deviations, and the relocation read stays at $0.65$ to $0.66$. Within these three forms, then, the findings on the primary section do not depend on the spill-over being section-wide and gene-agnostic; forms outside them are not tested.

\paragraph{A false-positive floor.} The model has no background term (\cref{app:limitations}). A fixed false-positive floor added to the mixture, one rate per section or one per cell scaled by its area, leaves every diagnostic of the primary section within $0.6$ standard deviations, but lowers its relocation read from $0.65$ to $0.50$ ($-8.3$) with the per-section floor and to $0.54$ ($-6.2$) with the area-scaled one, the largest moves in the table. On the TMA core the floor leaves the relocation read at $0.73$ and moves the agreement with type up by $2.4$ and the mirror down by $4.0$ standard deviations of a very narrow spread. The relocation read on the primary section therefore depends on the absence of a background term, and it is the read to treat with most caution there.

\paragraph{The response weight.} The operating point $\alphaw = 0.1$ lies far above the bound-equivalent value $1/\lbar$ (\cref{app:pathb}); it corresponds to $\alphaw = m/\lbar$ with $m$ of about $14$ on the primary section, $28$ on the TMA core and $143$ on the fresh-frozen section. Lowering it opens the per-cell channel. At $\alphaw = 1/\lbar$ the response's deviation from its prior mean carries $3.5\%$ of its variance on the primary section and $21\%$ on the TMA core, against $0.2\%$ at the operating point, and adds $0.015$ to $0.017$ nats per count to held-out reconstruction, against at most $0.001$. The cost is in the agreement of $\vz$ with type, which falls by $2.6$ and $2.8$ standard deviations ($0.684$ against $0.713$, $0.607$ against $0.621$), and by $2.9$ on the fresh-frozen section at $10/\lbar$ ($0.629$ against $0.697$). The lower weights raise the relocation read on the primary section ($0.71$ at $1/\lbar$) and on the fresh-frozen section ($0.75$ at $5/\lbar$ and $10/\lbar$), but not on the TMA core. The operating point therefore keeps the agreement with type at the price of a closed per-cell channel (\cref{app:planted-percell}). The deviation's share of variance and its held-out gain are read from the same fits as \cref{tab:sensitivity} but are not in the table.

\paragraph{The adversary.} Doubling the heads' update steps or their width, or averaging an ensemble of three heads, changes the nonlinear composition residual by at most $0.7$ standard deviations, and doubling the steps costs $4.3$ in agreement with type. Weighting the composition term equally with the image term, the ablation of the adopted weight of three, raises the residual from $4.6\%$ to $6.6\%$ ($+2.5$) and the mirror by $2.0$; a weight of five lowers the residual to $3.7\%$ at a cost of $2.1$ in agreement with type.

\paragraph{Without the intrinsic path.} The final configuration is refitted on all four sections with $\omega = 0$, which drops the intrinsic path and sets the factor $(1+\omega)$ on the $\vz$-divergence to one, from three seeds at the final fits' splits, and read as the final fits are. The prediction was set before the fits: without the path the allocation should shift toward $\vw$, so that the agreement of $\vz$ with type and its cycle $R^2$ fall, the agreement of $\vmu_w$ with type and the cycle $R^2$ of $\vw$ rise, and the within-type share of $\vw$'s variance changes. A read counts as moved when the mean over the three seeds lies outside the final fits' range over seeds. Two reads move on every section, with the two ranges disjoint: the NMI of $\vmu_w$ with the type rises ($0.42$ to $0.50$ on the primary section, $0.27$ to $0.32$ on the lung section, $0.33$ to $0.35$ on the fresh-frozen section, $0.29$ to $0.40$ on the TMA core), and the within-type share of $\vw$'s variance falls ($0.32$ to $0.19$, $0.43$ to $0.37$, $0.53$ to $0.49$, $0.53$ to $0.33$). The intrinsic state loses less. Its cycle $R^2$ falls on the fresh-frozen section, from $0.76$ to $0.70$ with disjoint ranges; on the primary section its agreement with type ($0.71$ to $0.67$) and its cycle $R^2$ ($0.52$ to $0.50$) fall by the mean only, the ranges overlapping; elsewhere both stay within the final fits' range. The cycle $R^2$ of $\vw$ does not rise on any section, and no read for which nothing was predicted, the probe's composition residual, the response's information about the niche and held-out reconstruction among them, moves with disjoint ranges. No move is opposite to the prediction. Without the path, then, the type enters $\vw$, while $\vz$ keeps most of what it carries: the path's role at the final configuration is to keep the type out of $\vw$. One final fit on the TMA core, refitted with the software used for the ablation, comes out close to the original but not identical (NMI of $\vz$ $0.616$ against $0.621$, just below the final fits' range); the moves above are larger than that.

\subsection{What Each Part of the Model Buys in Reconstruction}
\label{app:recon-modes}

Held-out reconstruction decodes a cell from its own posterior, so it reads the cell's counts twice, once in the encoder and once in the likelihood, and a single number of it says little. \Cref{tab:recon-modes} instead decodes the held-out cells of each fit in four ways and reports the differences, in nats per count. The \emph{type profile} is the empirical count profile of the cell's type over training cells. The \emph{context} decode uses nothing of the cell's own counts: $\vz$ is its type's mean over training cells and $\vw$ the prior mean $\mpsi(\vc_i, t_i)$, so what remains is the type, the context and the spill-over influx. The \emph{intrinsic} decode uses the cell's own $\vz$ with $\vw$ at the prior mean, and the \emph{full} decode its own $\vz$ and $\vw$. The spill-over influx and $\kappa$ are those of the fit in every decode.

Three readings follow. The cell's own intrinsic state carries most of what the model adds over the type profile. The context, which uses no counts of the cell, adds a little on three sections and nothing on the lung FFPE section. And the response's own posterior adds almost nothing beyond its prior mean, one part in a thousand or less, which is the operating point's design: in reconstruction the response reads as the context regression (\cref{app:pathb}); on subtype recovery the lung section is the exception (\cref{app:subtype}). The model fitted to the TMA core reads its serial section as it reads the core.
