\subsection{Readouts}
\label{app:readouts}

Every readout is taken at the accepted checkpoint of each fit. Unassigned cells are excluded as targets of every readout but remain neighbours, in the composition and in the spill-over influx. A readout is reported as the mean over the three seeds with the range; unless stated otherwise, its interval is from a bootstrap over $200\um$ squares of the section ($1{,}000$ draws, conditional on the fitted model and probes), and a table gives the envelope of the three seeds' intervals; for the reads of \cref{tab:headline}, this is \cref{tab:headline-full}.

\Cref{tab:headline} gives the model-quality reads of the final fits on every section, with the range over seeds; the main text's \cref{tab:headline-main} summarises them.

\paragraph{What the response is and is not.} The niche descriptor $\vc_i$ is deterministic, shared by co-located cells, and a conditioning variable. If a niche descriptor were all that was wanted, one would drop the latent and feed $\vc_i$ to the decoder directly, roughly the node-centric model of \citet{fischer2023}, which is simpler. The response $\vw_i$ is latent and per cell, and it earns its place because the direct path into its posterior (\cref{app:qw}) lets $\vB$ and $\mpsi$ be fitted against each cell's own residual rather than a niche-level average; per-cell anomaly scores, per-cell responses and counterfactuals with abduction are further uses it is designed for, and they are future work. At $\alphaw$ far above its bound-equivalent value the objective itself suppresses the divergence, so a small divergence shows how the objective is weighted, not that responses within a niche are homogeneous (\cref{app:pathb,app:planted-percell}).

\paragraph{Niches.} Where a readout needs discrete niches, they are the ten clusters of a $k$-means clustering of the neighbour composition $\vy_i$ of connected cells; isolated cells have none.

\paragraph{Diagnostics.} The mirror $R^2$ is the pooled coefficient of determination of a linear regression of $\vmu_z$ on the context $\vc_i$, both centred per type, on up to $30{,}000$ cells (\cref{sec:selection}). The response's information about the niche is a nearest-neighbour estimate \citep{ross2014} of the mutual information between the niche label and $\vmu_w$ on up to $20{,}000$ held-out cells, in nats, minus its mean over five permutations of the label within type. The cycle scores are the S and G2/M scores of the gene sets of \citet{tirosh2016}, intersected with the panel: for each set, the mean depth-normalised log expression of its genes minus that of control genes drawn from the same expression bins, computed with Scanpy \citep{wolf2018} from the cell's own segmented counts. They are a proxy for the cycle, not a measurement of it: the counts also hold transcripts spilled over from neighbours and expression induced by the environment, and both enter the scores (\cref{sec:results-z}). The cycling set is the tenth of held-out cells with the highest summed score, fixed once per section for every method, together with the training cells that score above its lowest member; the cycle $R^2$ is that of a ridge regression of both scores on the latent, each centred per type within the set, fitted on the set's training cells and scored on its held-out cells. The linear reference is the same regression on the leading $50$ principal components of the log-normalised counts.

\paragraph{Relocation.} A panel is a cell type and a pair of niches $A$ and $B$, each with at least $500$ cells that supply the model quantities and $100$ cells of the type to be scored. The tiles of the section are dealt into five spatial folds; one fold is scored, and the other four supply every model quantity. Per gene, the observed shift is the difference between the two niches in the logarithm of the type's mean depth-normalised counts. The prediction holds niche $A$'s intrinsic rate fixed and adds two channels: the response programmes, $\vB$ times the difference between the mean prior means $\mpsi(\vc_i, t)$ of the type's cells in $B$ and in $A$, and the spill-over, the change in the logarithm of the mixture of \cref{eq:gen-p} when $A$'s mean spill-over influx is replaced by $B$'s at the fit's $\kappa$. The prediction therefore moves the type-level prior mean to a new context with the intrinsic state held fixed; no cell's own posterior response enters it. Its score is the $R^2$ of the prediction against the observed shift, both centred over the genes expressed in both niches. The noise ceiling is the Spearman--Brown reliability of the observed shift, from the correlation between the shifts computed on two random halves of the scored cells, averaged over four splits. Only panels whose two niches differ by more than three standard deviations of composition along the line between them enter, and a panel is trusted when its ceiling is at least $0.5$ with at least $100$ genes scored. The fraction of the ceiling is the mean score over the trusted panels divided by their mean ceiling. Over all panels, trusted or not, the same ratio divides by near-zero ceilings and can exceed one, so it is reported only in \cref{tab:transport-heldout}. The programme and spill-over parts of \cref{tab:cellina-cf} are the same ratio for each channel alone; they are separate scores and do not add up. The interval draws half of the tiles without replacement, recomputes the observed shifts, scores and ceilings on the drawn half and rescales the spread of the draws to the full section. On planted sections with tile-clustered noise its coverage is $0.94$ to $0.96$ at ceilings of $0.82$ and $0.85$, but $0.78$ to $0.88$ at ceilings of $0.52$ to $0.63$, just above the trusted tier's threshold, so at the ceilings of real panels it is closer to an $80\%$ than a $95\%$ interval.

\paragraph{Single-cell relocation score (Read A) and nearest-twin advantage (twin margin).} On the same panels, without the trust filter, up to $2{,}000$ held-out cells of the type in niche $A$ are decoded from their own $\vmu_z$ at niche $B$'s mean prior response and mean influx (the relocated cloud) and at niche $A$'s own (the cloud left in place), and the type's held-out cells in $B$ are decoded at their own posterior means, context and influx (the target cloud). Read A is the gap closed, $(U - T)/(U - F)$, where $T$ and $U$ are the maximum mean discrepancies \citep{gretton2012} of the relocated cloud and of the cloud left in place from the target, under a Gaussian kernel on square-root profiles, and $F$ that between two random halves of the target; it is clipped to $[-1, 1]$ and summarised by its median over panels. Its reference replaces the relocated cloud by copies of the target's mean profile, and its interval resamples the tiles of the source cells. The twin margin matches each target cell to the relocated source cell nearest to it in $\vmu_z$, and reports by how much the median Hellinger distance from the target's own decode to its match is smaller than to a random relocated source cell. Because the decode is continuous in $\vz$, part of this margin follows from the construction.

\paragraph{Response programmes.} The programmes are the principal directions of the within-type-centred response that carry at least $1\%$ of its variance, rotated by varimax \citep{kaiser1958} in gene space; their number is the effective rank. The cross-seed cosine compares the first programme's gene signature between seeds, and the programme overlap of \cref{tab:breakdown-traj} compares the gene-space subspaces that the programmes of two fits span. For their localisation, each programme's absolute loadings over the genes expressed in at least $1\%$ of cells are compared between the members of four cellular-component sets of the Gene Ontology \citep{ashburner2000,go2023}, extracellular, plasma membrane, cytoplasm and nucleus, and the rest of the panel, by a Mann--Whitney test with Benjamini--Hochberg control \citep{benjamini1995} across sets and programmes within a fit; the effect is the rank-biserial correlation.

\paragraph{External criteria.} \emph{Signalling-gene share}, after MintFlow \citep{akbarnejad2025mintflow}: on the relocation panels, each gene's observed shift is split into its response part, its spill-over part and a remainder, and each gene's share of each part is accumulated over panels; the genes that are ligands or receptor subunits in a curated interaction database \citep{jin2025cellchat} are compared with the other genes of the panel by a Mann--Whitney test, with the rank-biserial correlation as the effect. \emph{Niche information of both latents}: the mutual information of the niche label with $\vmu_z$ and with $\vmu_w$ on $20{,}000$ held-out cells, by the nearest-neighbour estimator above and by a neural estimator \citep{belghazi2018}, each minus its mean over permutations within type. \emph{True and false axis}, after SIMVI \citep{dong2025simvi}, on the primary section only: cells are banded by the fraction of tumour cells among their $50$ nearest neighbours, in six bands. For each gene and type, the response-predicted shift per band, from the prior means of the type's cells, is correlated by Kendall's $\tau$ with the band index (the true axis) and with equal-count bins of the in-plane coordinate least correlated with the band index (the false axis). The same is done for shifts predicted from the intrinsic state and from depth, and for the observed shift. The false axis is not orthogonal to the bands: both in-plane coordinates correlate with the band index at about $0.41$.

\paragraph{Marker pairs.} After resolVI \citep{ergen2025}: twelve pairs of markers that should not be expressed together in one cell and six or seven control pairs that are, fixed per section before the read, are scored on held-out cells. A pair is double positive in the raw counts when both genes have a count; in the decode when both expected counts $\ell_i p_{ig}$ round to at least one; and in the corrected decode when both round to at least one after the expected spill-over influx $\kappa\ell_i\bar\rho_{ig}$ is removed, that is, from $(1-\kappa)\,\ell_i\rho_{ig}$. The marker-pair contrast, reported as a trajectory in \cref{tab:breakdown-traj}, is the change in the exclusive pairs' double-positive rate from the raw counts to the corrected decode, minus the same change for the control pairs. At $\kappa = 0$ the corrected decode is the decode.

\subsection{Merged Subtypes}
\label{app:subtype}

Within each lineage that absorbed at least two curated classes or clusters, those become sublabels, each needing at least $20$ training and $10$ held-out cells. Probes fitted on training tiles and scored on held-out tiles predict the sublabel from $\vmu_z$, from $\vmu_w$, from the prior mean $\mpsi(\vc_i, t_i)$ and from both latents: a ridge regression (primary) and, for the two latents, a small perceptron. Each sublabel is scored by its one-versus-rest AUC against the rest of its lineage, with a floor from five permutations of the sublabels within the lineage and a tile-bootstrap interval. The call, fixed before the first read: a channel recovers a sublabel when the lower end of its interval is above its floor; channel $a$ is far ahead of $b$ when the interval of their difference is above zero and $b$'s excess over its floor is at most half of $a$'s; the call is the majority over seeds. Only the curated sublabels of the primary section and of the TMA core carry an expected allegiance (a state to $\vz$, a location to the response); the clusters of the lung and fresh-frozen sections are read for information. On the primary section the malignant cells lining the cyst are their own lineage, so every channel reads them through the type, and their expected allegiance cannot be tested. On the primary section, tumour- and stroma-associated fibroblasts and endothelial cells are separated by the response, and equally by its prior mean, with a one-versus-rest AUC of $0.96$ to $0.99$, against $0.71$ to $0.72$ from $\vz$, on two seeds of three by the pre-set call; proliferating and inflammatory tumour cells are separated by $\vz$ with $0.96$ to $0.97$, against $0.61$ from the response, on every seed. VEGFA$^+$ tumour cells, a state that also occupies a region, are read from $\vz$ ($0.96$) and also from the response ($0.82$). On the TMA core, myofibroblasts among adventitial fibroblasts are read better from the response than from $\vz$ ($0.92$ against $0.77$); on the fresh-frozen section, three tumour-state clusters are read from both ($0.90$ to $0.96$ from $\vz$, $0.77$ to $0.86$ from the response). On the ovarian FFPE and fresh-frozen sections the response and its prior mean give nearly the same AUC for every sublabel (within $0.07$ and $0.01$), the per-cell channel being closed (\cref{sec:results-w}). On the TMA core they do too (within $0.06$), except for activated against venous endothelium, which the prior mean separates better than the response ($0.76$ against $0.59$, on the one seed where it can be graded); few lineages there have enough cells to grade. On the lung section the response separates clusters within three lineages better than its prior mean (AT1, $0.67$ against $0.55$; multiciliated cells, $0.71$ against $0.57$; one tumour cluster, $0.73$ against $0.59$), although its own deviation adds no more to reconstruction there than elsewhere (\cref{tab:recon-modes}); $\vz$ separates all three better still ($0.90$). On that section the response readouts are therefore not only readouts of the context regression.
