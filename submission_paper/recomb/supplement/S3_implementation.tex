\paragraph{The adversary's targets.} The image target $\ephi_i$ of \cref{eq:adv} is a soft membership of $\vPhi_i$ in $\Ephi$ image niches, from $k$-means on its leading principal components, never on the raw high-dimensional embedding, where distances are noise-dominated. We set $\Ephi = K$ so that the two heads face comparable difficulty; soft rather than hard memberships give a smoother target with no boundary artefacts. The composition head is weighted by $\lambda_y = 3$, in the encoder term and in the heads' own loss, because composition is the channel of the spill-over confound: at equal weights the probe finds more residual niche signal in $\vz$ ($6.6\%$ of the composition's within-type variance against $4.6\%$ on the primary section; \cref{app:sensitivity}). The adversary and the probe thus target the image through different summaries, discrete memberships and continuous components respectively: the memberships suit a classification head, the components the closed-form penalty of \cref{eq:S-penalty} and its probe.

\paragraph{Warm-up, diagnostics and seeds.} The response divergence is ramped because without the ramp the conditional prior can fail to open on a given seed: the posterior of $\vw$ collapses onto a prior that ignores $\vc$ within the first ten epochs and never recovers. The collapse shows as a divergence that stays at zero and as no information between $\vw$ and the niche on held-out cells, and such a fit is discarded and re-seeded (\cref{app:planted}); the converged objective is unchanged. The running maximum of the type agreement ratchets upward, so a checkpoint that buys reconstruction at the cost of type structure is rejected rather than traded off. Held-out cells are entire spatial tiles, never random cells, because spatial autocorrelation passes the answer through neighbouring cells on both sides of a random split \citep{roberts2017}. Five diagnostics accompany every fit: (i) the within-type mirror $R^2$ of $\vz_i$ on $\vc_i$ against a within-type permuted control (\cref{app:mirror}); (ii) the response divergence per dimension (\cref{app:qw}); (iii) the held-out probe of \cref{eq:probe}; (iv) the $\vz$--type agreement, whose collapse marks the marginal-invariance failure; and (v) two checks that $\vz$ has not collapsed onto the label, the ratio $\I(\vz;t)/H(t)$ with the within-type variance of $\vmu_z$, and the gain in held-out reconstruction of decoding from the cell's own $\vz$ rather than its type's mean. On the final fits $\I(\vz;t)/H(t)$ is $0.82$ to $0.95$, the within-type share of the variance of $\vmu_z$ is $0.60$ to $0.72$, and the gain is $0.06$ to $0.17$ nats per count (\cref{tab:recon-modes}): $\vz$ retains the type almost fully without collapsing onto it. The objective is not unimodal in the seed: repeated fits of one configuration can fall into different basins. Each seed also redraws the held-out split, the image principal components and the image-niche clustering, so the range over seeds covers these choices as well.

\subsection{Tiles and the Two-Hop Halo}
\label{app:tiles}

Because $\rhobar_i$ needs $\vrho_j$, hence $\vw_j$, hence $\vc_j$, hence $\nbr{j}$, a training step for cell $i$ reaches two graph hops although the attention layer reaches one. The neighbours' responses $\vw_j$ are posterior draws from the same batched pass, under the stop-gradient. Computing the influx from the baseline programme alone, $\vrho_j \approx \softmax(a(\vz_j))$, would avoid the neighbours' niches but errs in the direction that matters: neighbours share niches, so dropping $\vw_j$ strips the local programme out of $\rhobar_i$, and what the spill-over term then fails to absorb is explained by $\vw_i$ instead, inflating apparent spatial effects. Without the stop-gradient, cell $j$'s clean composition would be trained to absorb cell $i$'s residual through $\rhobar_i$, so $\vrho_j$ would stop being $j$'s own profile (\cref{app:amplification}).

Batching by contiguous spatial tile makes the two hops cheap, because the halo around a tile is shared rather than disjoint; scattered seeds would cost many times more nodes. A tile's cells are the \emph{seeds}; their graph neighbours form \emph{ring one}, for which the niche descriptor, the response posterior and the clean composition are computed; the neighbours of ring one form \emph{ring two}, for which only the type is looked up. Both rings are built from the same edge list as $\beta$, never by a distance query: a cell across a pruned edge is not in ring one even if a radius search would return it, and if the halo and the kernel disagreed the spill-over influx would be silently wrong. Attention edges are restricted to destinations in the seeds and ring one, since a ring-two cell's niche descriptor would normalise over a truncated neighbourhood, and the loss is evaluated on seeds only. One consequence should be stated: the ring of a training tile can contain cells of a held-out tile, whose counts then enter training as inputs under a stop-gradient, with no loss and no gradient, that is, transductively. On our sections $0.8$ to $1.9\%$ of training seeds have a held-out neighbour, and $4.8$ to $10\%$ of held-out cells enter some training step in this way; within two hops, where only a cell's type is used, the share of seeds is $1.8$ to $4\%$. We state the exposure rather than add a buffer, which would remove the same seeds. \Cref{fig:batching} shows the tiles, the held-out split and the halo of one training tile on the primary section.
