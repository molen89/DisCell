\subsection{Data and Preprocessing}
\label{sec:data}

\paragraph{Sections.}
The primary section is a publicly available formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded ovarian carcinoma section profiled on the Xenium platform \needsource with a $5{,}001$-gene panel plus $100$ custom targets: $407{,}120$ segmented cells after removing four without a usable boundary polygon, a median of $178$ transcripts and $147$ detected genes per cell (\cref{app:sections}). A second section \pending{lung carcinoma section, same panel family, unsupervised cluster labels} is used to test whether the decomposition transfers to a tissue with no curated annotation.

\paragraph{Labels.}
On the ovarian section $t_i$ is the vendor-supplied curated annotation with $K = 18$ classes, one of which is an ``unassigned'' class that we keep as a type in the model but exclude from all biological readouts. As a label-set control we refit the ovarian section with an unsupervised graph-based clustering ($K = 26$) in place of the curated annotation (\cref{app:graphclust}) \pending{multi-seed}.

\paragraph{Graph, kernel, image embedding.}
Delaunay adjacency with Voronoi faces, pruned at $40\um$; kernel $\beta$ with $\tau = 20\um$; image embedding from a pathology foundation model for multiplexed immunofluorescence \citep{kronos2025} applied to a $128\um$ patch at $0.5\um$ per pixel in which a $25\um$ disc around the cell centroid is masked. Every choice in this pipeline, including the measurement that a contact-based graph is degenerate on this platform, the selection of the mask radius from the distribution of cell covering radii, and the test that the masked embedding carries no cell-type information beyond neighbour homophily, is reported in \cref{app:data}.

\paragraph{Splits.}
Cells are partitioned into contiguous rectangular tiles of at most 4{,}096 cells by recursive median splits; $15\%$ of tiles are held out and serve every held-out readout and the stopping rule. There is no third split. \todo{decide whether an untouched test set of tiles is required for the final claims}

\subsection{Operating Point and Calibration}
\label{sec:operating-point}

\paragraph{Goal.} Fix every weight in \cref{eq:objective} other than $\kappa$ on the primary section before the sweep, by the probe protocol of \cref{sec:invariance}, so that the sweep varies one thing.

\paragraph{Protocol.} Short fits at $\kappa = 0.1$ scanning $\alphaa$ under the closed-form penalty, then $\alphaw$ and $\omega$; a convergence-length check of the closed-form operating point with both probe families; escalation to the adversary and a scan of its strength and update schedule; the operating point chosen at the crossing of held-out $\dCE$ and $\vz$--type agreement. The full calibration history, including the two knife-edges in $\alphaw$ and $\alphaa$ and the observation that an under-trained adversary is a placebo, is in \cref{app:calibration}.

\subsection{What \texorpdfstring{$\vz$}{z} Carries and What \texorpdfstring{$\vw$}{w} Carries}
\label{sec:allegiance}

\paragraph{Goal.} Every analysis here is a target variable with a known allegiance, probed identically from both latents against reference rows: a within-type permutation floor, a depth-only baseline (the probe from $\log \ell$ alone, since depth varies across tissue), and, for expression-derived targets, a linear reference from the leading principal components of the counts. The claim is always the asymmetry between the two latents, never one number. Spatial-block cross-validation is used throughout.

\paragraph{Intrinsic target: cell-cycle phase.} Continuous S and G2/M scores from standard gene sets \citep{tirosh2016}, regressed within type from $\vmu_z$, $\vmu_w$ and the references on the types with the highest fraction of proliferating cells (\cref{app:validation}). \pending{multi-seed numbers}

\paragraph{Spatial targets.} (i) A discrete niche label from $k$-means on neighbour composition ($10$ niches), predicted per type by class-balanced multinomial regression from each latent; (ii) per-dimension Moran's I \citep{moran1950} of each latent after per-type centring, against a permutation null, with collapsed response dimensions flagged; (iii) a principal-curve pseudotime in each latent space and its agreement with the tissue gradient after partialling out type; (iv) distance to anatomical landmarks (vasculature, compact smooth-muscle structures, the tumour--stroma interface) in a mid-range band where one-hop context cannot see the landmark, with an additional baseline from raw neighbour composition because the landmark sets are themselves type-defined (\cref{app:validation}). \pending{multi-seed numbers}
