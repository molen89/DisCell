## A. The model as written no longer matches the model that runs

**A1. Attention sources are types only.** Spec 9 §2/§4.1: `x_src = onehot(t_j)`,
no `sg μ_z`; `c_i` is a pure function of data (types, graph, image). Paper
locations to change: method eq. `context-src` and the paragraph after it
(`μ_z(x_j, t_j)` under stop-gradient); implementation table rows "Type query"
(width K + d_z → K) and "Attention sources" ([onehot, sg μ_z] → onehot);
batching paragraph ("neighbour codes μ_z(x_j) ... enter under a stop-gradient"
no longer exists, only ρ_j does); ring-2 description ("only μ_z is computed to
serve as attention sources" → type lookup only); the mirror paragraph's "a value
channel remains ... neighbours' codes resemble the cell's" (that channel is
gone; the within-type mirror R² is still monitored, 0.044–0.046 vs permuted
0.016); intro sentence on the type query gains "and reads only neighbour
types". Rationale appendix table "Three routes" row "Mirror" → query and source
side.

*Caution on the justification.* Spec 9 §4.1 and §7.2 motivate type_only with a
mechanism (m_ψ reconstructing the cell from neighbours' μ_z; "the source-side
head"). The devlog (2026-09-11, w-mirror certification) falsified that mechanism
twice, and `spec_deviations.md` records the case as **empirical only**: the
3-seed ablation improved held-out recon (+0.006 at seed 0), NMI, mirror and
cycle_w with w's spatial rows unchanged, and the high-recon family it exposed is
a better optimum, not a pathology. The paper should state the empirical case and
not import spec 9's mechanistic paragraph. Spec 9 and the devlog disagree here;
flag to the architect.

**A2. Halo overhead. — APPLIED 2026-09-21.** Table quotes 21 % / 9.7 % measured with enc_z running on
ring 2. Spec 9 §4.5 says ring 2 now costs a lookup only and to re-measure, but
the code still encodes every node (posterior_z over seeds ∪ ring1 ∪ ring2; the
ring-2 μ_z is simply unused under type_only). Either keep the numbers as "for
the implementation as run" or implement the skip and re-measure. Spec/code
mismatch to note in `spec_deviations.md`.

**A3. Budget and model selection. — APPLIED 2026-09-21.** Table: "cosine annealing over 200 epochs,
patience 20; 7–23 min". Reference fits use 500 epochs / patience 40; the pinned
model is battery-selected from a seed triple (recon −7.19 vs −7.25 / −7.23); the
strong-family optimum is reached in 1–2 of 3 seeds at 500 epochs and never at
200 (sweep3 κ = 0.1: −7.2562 ± 0.0011). The model-selection section must add
the protocol: seed ensemble, selection by the full battery (recon + cycle_z +
allegiance + probes), never by recon alone; 200/20 is the sweep budget only.
One bad member of the family exists (alphaw_0.03_s2, cycle_z 0.354), which is
why the battery, not recon, selects.

**A4. Identifiability paragraph: add the per-type translation gauge. — APPLIED 2026-09-21.** For any
per-type constant μ_t, (a(z) − Bμ_t, w + μ_t) is the same model (m_ψ takes t,
KL_w is against m_ψ, term (b) uses m_ψ). Measured offsets are 4–10× the
within-type spread; coupled weight decay moves the gauge into w and makes it
worse (issues V12). Consequences: per-type ‖w‖ is not a context-dependence read
(B4); any "clean profile" decodes at a reference context, never at w = 0.

**A5. "Not built" row, method text and conclusion. — APPLIED 2026-09-21.** Type-level counterfactual
transport is built and run (Δ̂program + Δ̂leak with z fixed); only per-cell
abduction remains future work. "That machinery is not exercised in this paper"
→ exercised at the type level (C1). Nuclear/extranuclear counts now exist on all
five datasets (κ estimation still not built; marker-set ceiling still not
computed).

**A6. Options that exist, default off. — APPLIED 2026-09-21.** Leak-subtracted encoder input
x̃ = x − κℓρ̄ (architect-approved spec change gated on results, near-neutral on
three seeds, spec §7.13 not yet patched); attention sink (exact no-op on a
Voronoi graph, half-point below one neighbour); Adam weight decay. Mention x̃ as
the amortisation-gap ablation (B6); the sink at most one sentence in the graph
rationale (count blindness costs nothing because degree ≈ 6 everywhere).

**A7. Implementation-choices table. — APPLIED 2026-09-21.** Add rows: type_only sources (empirical
case), reference budget vs sweep budget, battery selection, int16 resident
counts for the 1.16 M-cell slide. The caption "as used in the experiments" now
means type_only.

## B. Numbers and claims that are stale or retracted

**B1. Canonical runs changed.** Every quoted number comes from `reference_best`
(type_z) and sweep2. Canonical now: `ablation_gat_type_only_s1` (pinned) and
sweep3 (type_only, 6 κ × 3 seeds, 200 epochs; table in `docs/handover.md` §4.2).
Replace in: low-degree strata, B stability, ‖w‖, w at the operating point,
graphclust control. New for the graphclust control: the ovarian graphclust run's
probe re-fitted against the *curated* labels reads ΔCE 0.061 vs 0.004 with the
floor at −0.056: coarser training labels leave finer-label niche information in
z. That is the label-granularity cost the invariance section predicts and should
be in the paper.

**B2. "α_w is a knife-edge" (calibration round 2; experiments "two
knife-edges").** Under the adversary the closed-form knife-edge does not
reproduce (NMI 0.631 at α_w = 0.03). What replaced it, from the 9-run α_w study:
α_w < 0.1 is seed-bistable (recon spread 0.08 vs ± 0.005 at 0.1); the high-recon
family is a better optimum by every instrument; w is context-only at every
tested α_w (prior-R² ≥ 0.996); the per-cell channel cannot be opened by lowering
α_w alone. α_w = 0.05 under type_only is a live candidate (a second context axis
becomes seed-stable there, axis cosine 0.77–0.90) awaiting the architect. Rewrite
round 2 as history plus the study; drop the "knife-edge" wording. The synthetic
appendix's "knife-edge" lesson also needs the caveat that the doc-10 arm-0 world
did not reproduce the M3 cliff with the invariance penalty active.

**B3. w rank, programmes, B stability.** app:w-prior's "first two PCs carry
85 % / 15 %" stands, but the reading changes: cov(μ_w) has effective rank 1–2
at α_w = 0.1, rank 3 at 0.05, rank 4 at ≤ 0.03, in the prior field itself; the
rank is a (d_w, α_w, budget) property of the optimiser, not of the tissue. The
d_w ablation {2, 3, 6, 8} × 3 seeds is done → keep d_w = 6 (d_w = 8 costs
cycle_w 0.008–0.020). app:B-stability's matched-column correlation (Kuhn
matching) is a metric artefact of the four null columns (issues V11): replace
with shift-space overlap of μ_w·B (0.97–0.99 across seeds) plus effective rank;
the κ = 0 → κ > 0 drop (0.70 → 0.45) is still unexplained and must be said so.
Sweep readouts paragraph: "stability of B after optimal column matching" →
shift-space overlap. Any "N programmes" statement names d_w, α_w and budget.

**B4. app:w-norm withdrawn.** The per-type ‖μ_w‖ ranking (VEGFA⁺ tumour on top,
"16 of 18 runs") is the offset gauge of A4 (Spearman −0.41 against the
within-type-centred ranking). Replace with the centred spread (macrophages,
SOX2-OT⁺ tumour, T/NK most context-modulated; VEGFA⁺ tumour least) or drop.
Remove "per-type magnitude of the response" from the sweep readouts and retire
`questions_and_issues.md` item 19.

**B5. KL_w is a snapshot of a chase.** app:w-prior's per-dimension divergence
(2e-4 to 1.5e-3) is a checkpoint value; KL_w oscillates 20–100× between
evaluations five epochs apart in every seed (s0's best checkpoint sits on a
spike). Quote KL_w over the trajectory. Per-cell KL_w has no heavy tail (top 1 %
of cells carry 8 %): the evidence for "anomaly score deactivated".

**B6. Amortisation gap: a limitation the paper lacks.** q(z | x, t) cannot see
ρ̄_i, so the per-cell posterior mean inherits what leaked into x_i; the leak is
removed on the decoder side only. The counts-level correction x̃ was tested on
three seeds: recon identical (± 0.001), niche-z residual −0.006 mean (2/3 seeds
in the predicted direction), NMI −0.017, w rows unmoved. Add to limitations; it
bears on any per-cell decontamination reading of μ_z.

**B7. Niche-z residual.** Already flagged (item 7). Pinned numbers: z 0.654 vs
ℓ 0.585 vs w 0.767, κ-reducible (+0.062 → +0.039), triage benign (hot z dims
y-R² ≤ 3.6 %). Update the numbers; the framing stands.

**B8. Landmarks.** Pinned run: interface mid-band w 0.054 vs y-baseline 0.036
(paper: 0.069 / 0.042 from `reference_best`); vasculature null unchanged. Use
one run's numbers throughout.

**B9. "z beats the linear reference ~2×"** is slide-specific. On the GSE315411
core: z 0.478 vs reference 0.497 same-section, 0.508 vs 0.473 held-out. State it
as an ovarian/lung observation, with the depth/label-granularity reading.

**B10. The exposure/covariate reading of c.** Neighbour-dose experiment on the
pinned run: under natural variation the image channel carries as much of w's
context-dependence as composition for immune receivers and 1.5–2.5× more for
TAFs and tumour cells, and the two are not additive in m_ψ. So roughly half
(immune) to a third (stromal/tumour) of w's context-dependence is
interventionable; a transport counterfactual that changes composition but
carries each niche's real Φ mixes an intervention with a description. Also:
count invariance is exact under the softmax (c sees fractions; degree ≈ 6 makes
this cheap here), and the isolated flag is an unfitted regime (899 cells; m_ψ
there is an extrapolation, 10–17 gene-space units from the one-neighbour
state). Add to the geometry paragraph and to the transport caveats.
