\section{SYNTHETIC RECOVERY}
\label{app:synthetic}

\paragraph{Goal.} Certify, on data simulated from the model's own generative story, that the inference procedure recovers the planted intrinsic states, responses and gene programmes, that the invariance penalty keeps the planted niche out of $\vz$, and that leakage produces the confound in the counts that the leakage sweep exists to address.

\paragraph{Generative process.} $N = 6{,}000$ cells uniformly in a $1{,}000\um$ box; $K = 8$ types assigned by a soft Voronoi partition around random anchors with Gumbel noise so that types cluster in space; the graph, kernel, composition and rings built by the same procedure as for real data from a Delaunay triangulation, with unit face lengths so that $\beta \propto \exp(-d/\tau)$ only; $\vz_i \sim \N(\mathbf{m}_{t_i}, 0.5^2\vI)$ with type means $\mathbf{m}_t \sim \N(\mathbf{0}, 1.5^2\vI)$; a planted response $\vw_i = \mathbf{M}^\top\vy_i + 0.3\,\boldsymbol\varepsilon_i$ linear in the true neighbour composition; $\log\vrho_i = \mathbf{A}\vz_i + \vB\vw_i$ (up to normalisation) with standard-normal loadings; $\rhobar_i$ the kernel-weighted neighbour average; $\vp_i$ the renormalised mixture at the planted $\kappa$; totals $\ell_i \sim \max(\Pois(150), 20)$; $\vx_i \sim \Mult(\ell_i, \vp_i)$; and a planted image descriptor $\vPhi_i = \mathbf{P}^\top\vy_i + 0.4\,\boldsymbol\varepsilon'_i$ in eight dimensions. $G = 60$ genes, planted $\dz = 4$, $\dw = 2$.

\paragraph{Fit.} A reduced model ($\dz = 8$, $\dw = 2$, hidden width $128$, attention width $16$) with the closed-form penalty ($\alphaa = 0.02$), $\alphaz = 0.007$, $\alphaw = 0.1$, $\omega = 1$, tiles of $512$ cells, $600$ epochs with cosine annealing, and the planted $\kappa$ given to the model. Two worlds: $\kappa = 0$, in which every pathway is identifiable, and $\kappa = 0.2$.

\paragraph{Readouts.} Type agreement of $\vmu_z$ against a reference from $K$-means on the leading principal components of the log-normalised counts (what the counts alone support) and against the planted $\vz$; the first canonical correlation between $\vmu_w$ and the planted response; the largest principal-angle cosine between the column spaces of the fitted and planted loadings (a random two-dimensional subspace of $\R^{60}$ scores about $0.3$); and the within-type coefficient of determination of the centred composition on $\vmu_z$, against the uncontrolled value.

\paragraph{Result.} \pending{single seed; to be replaced} At $\kappa = 0.2$ the type agreement is $0.62$ against a count-based reference of $0.77$ (the planted $\vz$ itself gives $0.97$, unreachable at $150$ counts); at $\kappa = 0$ the response canonical correlation is $0.80$ and the loading cosine $0.83$. The loadings are seed-bistable under a planted leak: at $\kappa = 0.2$ the cosine ranges from $0.15$ to $0.83$ across seeds at one configuration, while at $\kappa = 0$ it is stable ($0.65$, $0.83$). This is the confound of \cref{sec:generative} observed in silico, and it is the reason the sweep on real data uses several seeds per grid point and the reason no gene-programme claim is made from a single run. \pending{ten seeds per world; recovery as a function of the assumed $\kappa$ when it is mis-specified relative to the planted one; a world with $G$ closer to the panel size}

\paragraph{Simulator checks.} Leakage raises the kernel-weighted cosine between a cell's composition and its neighbours' (by more than $0.02$ at $\kappa = 0.4$ relative to $0$); the planted response is linearly predictable from composition ($R^2 > 0.7$); the mixture is exactly the renormalised convex combination and reduces to $\vrho$ for isolated cells.

\paragraph{Lessons that shaped the objective.} Four facts were established here before any fit on real data. Under the $1/\ell$ scaling, unit weights collapse $\vz$ (divergence to zero, agreement $0.21$); the bound-equivalent weight is $\approx 1/\lbar$. The response weight is a knife-edge: near $1/\lbar$ the direct path lets $\vw$ steal identity (agreement $0.12$); at $0.1$ and above the response pins to its prior. The covariance of the composition block is singular on the simplex, which is why one column is dropped and the penalty is computed on correlations. And at a moderate penalty strength the loading cosine rose from $0.56$ to $0.83$ while the within-type niche information in $\vz$ fell, which is the penalty doing its job.
