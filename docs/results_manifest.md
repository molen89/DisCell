(adv_ladder/adv_confirm/wcollapse* remain quotable as the *historical decision record* that produced the final settings — just not relabeled as "final-configuration sensitivity.")
- **Old-label numbers** — any cycle_z / cycle_w / NMI / axis-test number computed under the pre-lineage 18-class (ovarian) or graphclust labels is not comparable to the lineage-label numbers and must not be mixed into the same table (devlog 2026-09-27: "old-label and lineage cycle_z numbers are not comparable and must not be quoted as a model change"). Concretely: `envelope_table_at_best.md`/`envelope_table.md` (without `_lineage`/`_ci` suffix), `baseline_battery.md`/`baseline_battery.json` (without `_lineage`), `cycle_target_reference_graphclust.json`, `external_mi_quadrant_reference_graphclust.json`, `external_signalling_share_reference_graphclust.*`, `ego_masking.{csv,json}` (18-class) — all superseded, per `docs/handover.md` §13 "Stale artefacts to know about."
- **The 2-sd guard verdicts** for the per-block invariance probe (the original `|excess| ≤ 2×floor sd` pass rule set 2026-09-24 morning) — withdrawn same day as "the wrong scale" (floor sd is estimation noise, not a tolerance; every latent including near-type-code MintFlow would "fail"). Replaced by the ≤25%-of-uncontrolled guard. Any verdict phrased as "passes/fails the 2-sd guard" is invalid.
- **Transport fraction-of-ceiling CIs from the bootstrap-with-replacement round** (2026-09-27 early legs, before the half-tile-subsampling fix) — these intervals excluded their own point estimate on every panel and were explicitly declared invalid ("A bootstrap-with-replacement interval is not valid for a ratio to a noise ceiling"). Only the half-tile-subsampling `bootstrap_ci.json` entries (method: "half-tile subsampling") are valid; note their coverage caveat (~0.8, not 0.95, at real-panel ceilings).
- **MintFlow recon cell B-mf1** — `baseline_battery.py`'s MintFlow reconstruction value is identical (−5.035 / −5.018) across different fits and transfers, flagged as likely a degenerate normalisation or wrong decode; `docs/issues.md` marks it "do not quote until inspected," still open.
